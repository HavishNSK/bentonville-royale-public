Privacy

Bentonville Royale · last updated November 2026

Bentonville Royale is a free browser game. This page explains, in plain words, what the game stores about you, why, and what you can do about it. We keep as little as we can.

You can play without an account

Guests play without signing in. A guest's name, settings and look are saved only in your own browser (local storage), not on our server.

If you make an account

  • What we keep: your username, your coins, the items you own and wear, your wins and eliminations, your friends list, and when the account was made.
  • Passwords are stored only as a salted hash, never as the password itself.
  • Google or Clever sign-in: we keep only the account id they give us, so we can find your account next time. We do not keep your e-mail address.
  • Your username, wins and eliminations can appear on the public RANKS boards.

Playing online

  • Who's online: while the game is open, your display name and whether you are in the lobby or a match are shared with other players. This is not saved.
  • Global chat: messages are public to everyone online, filtered for swear words, links, e-mail addresses and phone numbers, and only the last 30 are kept in memory. Never share personal information in chat.
  • Ideas & bugs board: posts are public. To stop spam we briefly store the network address a post came from; it is never shown to anyone.

Ads

Ads help keep the game free. Short ads can play between matches, and you can choose to watch an ad for in-game coins. Ads never play during a match.

  • On our own website, ads come from Google AdSense. Because many players are young, we ask Google for non-personalized, child-safe ads only: no ads based on your interests or browsing. Google may still use cookies or similar technology to show and measure ads and to stop fraud. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services.
  • On CrazyGames, ads are run by CrazyGames under their privacy policy.
  • Visitors in the EU, UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before ads use cookies.

Analytics

Our website counts anonymous page views with Vercel Web Analytics, which does not use cookies and does not identify you.

Kids

The game is made for a general audience that includes young players. We do not ask for anyone's age, real name, e-mail address, address or phone number, and we do not sell personal information. Parents or guardians who want a child's account deleted can ask us (see below) and we will remove it.

Deleting your data

Ask us to delete your account and we will remove it and everything tied to it. Guest data lives in your browser: clearing this site's data removes it.

Contact

Post on the IDEAS & BUGS board in the game (title screen › PATCH NOTES) and mark it as a privacy request, or reach the developer through the game's official pages.
