# UI art credits

## Title, friend-lobby and loading backdrop

- `public/ui/lobby-dusk.svg` (in use): Bentonville at dusk, a flat vector drawing in the game's own palette (sun-gold sun behind the 3D line-up, the courthouse dome, the square's storefronts and the water tower). Drawn in code for this project; no outside sources.
- `public/ui/lobby-bg.jpg` (kept, no longer shown on the title): the Bentonville water-tower illustration (meteor, explosions, the town and the Crystal Bridges ponds). Original artwork made for this game by the project owner, Shehryar, and part of this project.

## Update flyer images

- `public/ui/news/bb-*.jpg`: screenshots captured inside the game (HUD hidden) for the v0.17.0 "BOMBS and Bridges" update flyer.
- `public/ui/news/bb-item-*.png`: renders of the game's own in-code models (`grenadeModel`, `smokeGrenadeModel` and `launcherModel` in `src/game/Grenades.js`) on transparent backgrounds.
- What appears in them (the map, the Crystal Bridges site, the statues and the paintings) keeps its own credits: see [map-data.md](map-data.md) and [crystal-bridges.md](crystal-bridges.md).
